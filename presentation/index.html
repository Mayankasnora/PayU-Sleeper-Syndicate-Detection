Critical Methodological Note • Theoretical Designation & Occam's Razor
Scientific Rigor & Cost Governance
⚠ Why We Designate This As "Theoretical":

Although rigorously benchmarked on our 52-week longitudinal simulation, synthetic datasets exhibit clean mathematical boundaries where multiple models achieve near-perfect ~100% Precision and Recall. Real-world payment ecosystems harbor noisy merchant camouflage, delayed chargeback arrivals, and human data errors. An actual production dataset is strictly necessary to measure true architectural divergence with statistical confidence.

✓ Occam's Razor & Avoiding Excessive Complexity:

Selecting overly complex architectures (e.g., Graph Neural Networks or deep multi-modal ensembles) substantially inflates cloud compute costs, deployment friction, and gateway latency without delivering meaningful real-world performance gains over simpler models (as proven in our benchmark where lightweight hybrids matched or exceeded heavy alternatives at a fraction of serving costs).
